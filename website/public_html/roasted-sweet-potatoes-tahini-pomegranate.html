🍠 Roasted Sweet Potatoes With Tahini and Pomegranate

Caramelized sweet potato rounds finished with honey-lemon tahini, juicy pomegranate seeds and toasted sesame. Original recipe by Yossy Arefi for NYT Cooking

← Back to Recipes
Prep Time15 minutes
Cook Time40 minutes
Total Time55 minutes
Servings6–8

Ingredients

  • 2½ pounds sweet potatoes, about 6–7 small, peeled and cut into ½-inch-thick rounds
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • Kosher salt, such as Diamond Crystal, and black pepper
  • ¼ cup tahini, well stirred
  • 2 tablespoons water
  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • ¼ cup pomegranate seeds
  • 1 tablespoon toasted sesame seeds

Instructions

1
Heat the oven to 375°F with a rack in the center. Place the sweet potato rounds on a large rimmed sheet pan, add the olive oil and season with salt and pepper. Toss well, then spread into a single layer.
2
Roast for 35–45 minutes, turning the rounds halfway through, until tender and caramelized at the edges.
3
While the potatoes roast, whisk the tahini, water, honey, lemon juice, ½ teaspoon salt and ¼ teaspoon black pepper until smooth.
4
Arrange the roasted sweet potatoes on a serving platter and serve warm or at room temperature. Just before serving, drizzle with the tahini sauce and scatter the pomegranate and toasted sesame seeds over the top.

💡 Cook's Tips

  • Keep the slices even: Uniform ½-inch rounds roast at the same rate.
  • Use one layer: Give the potatoes room on the pan so their edges caramelize instead of steaming.
  • Turn halfway: Flipping exposes both sides to the hot pan for more even browning.
  • Stir the tahini first: Tahini can separate in the jar; mix it well before measuring.
  • Finish at serving time: Add the sauce, pomegranate and sesame just before serving for the best contrast of creamy, juicy and crisp textures.
  • Nut alternative: Toasted chopped nuts may replace the sesame seeds.
