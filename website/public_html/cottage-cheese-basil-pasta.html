🧀 Creamy Cottage Cheese Basil Pasta

A light and creamy pasta dish made with cottage cheese, fresh basil, and garlic. Original Recipe

English Español
← Back to Recipes
Prep Time 10 minutes
Cook Time 15 minutes
Total Time 25 minutes
Servings 4

Ingredients

  • 1 pound pasta (penne, farfalle, or your favorite shape)
  • 2 cups cottage cheese (whole milk or low-fat)
  • 1 cup fresh basil leaves, loosely packed
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/4 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
  • Zest of 1 lemon
  • 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Red pepper flakes (optional)
  • Reserved pasta water (about 1/2 cup)

Instructions

1
Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the pasta and cook according to package directions until al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup of pasta water before draining.
2
While the pasta cooks, add the basil, garlic, and 1/4 cup of the olive oil to a food processor. Pulse until you have a chunky pesto-like mixture.
3
Transfer the basil mixture to a large bowl. Add the cottage cheese, lemon zest, lemon juice, and remaining olive oil. Stir until well combined. Season with salt and pepper.
4
Add the drained pasta to the cottage cheese mixture and toss gently to coat. If the sauce is too thick, add reserved pasta water a little at a time until you reach your desired consistency.
5
Top with grated Parmesan cheese and red pepper flakes if desired. Serve immediately.

💡 Chef's Tips

  • Cottage Cheese Quality: Use whole milk cottage cheese for the creamiest sauce, or low-fat if you prefer. Avoid non-fat for best results.
  • Fresh Basil Essential: Fresh basil is crucial for this dish. Avoid dried basil if possible.
  • Lemon Brightness: The lemon zest and juice add brightness to the mild cottage cheese. Don't skip them.
  • Pasta Water is Key: The starchy pasta water helps create a silky sauce. Add it gradually to achieve the right consistency.
  • Gentle Tossing: Toss gently to avoid breaking up the pasta. The sauce should coat each strand delicately.
  • Make Ahead: Prepare the sauce up to 2 hours in advance. Toss with hot pasta just before serving.
  • Protein Addition: Add grilled chicken, shrimp, or fresh peas for extra protein and vegetables.
