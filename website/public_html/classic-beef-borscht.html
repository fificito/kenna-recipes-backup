🥣 Classic Beef Borscht

A hearty beet soup with tender beef, cabbage, potatoes, carrots, and a deeply flavored tomato-beet vegetable base. Original recipe by Shinee Davaakhuu of Sweet & Savory

English Español
← Back to Recipes
Prep Time15 minutes
Cook Time45 minutes
Total Time1 hour
Servings8

For the Beef Broth

  • 1 pound (450 g) beef, cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 10 cups cold water
  • 2 teaspoons coarse kosher salt, divided, plus more to taste

For the Zajarka Vegetable Base

  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, pressed or minced
  • 2 medium beets
  • 2 medium carrots
  • 1 medium yellow onion
  • 4 tablespoons tomato paste

For the Soup

  • 2 medium Yukon Gold or russet potatoes
  • 2 celery ribs, optional
  • ¼ medium cabbage, thinly sliced, about 2–3 cups
  • 1 bay leaf
  • Freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • ¼ cup chopped fresh parsley or dill
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • Sour cream and bread, for serving

Instructions

1
Place the beef, cold water, and 1 teaspoon salt in a large pot or 5-quart Dutch oven. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, skimming away the foam that rises to the surface for a clearer broth.
2
Reduce the heat to medium and simmer the beef for about 45 minutes.
3
Meanwhile, wash and peel the vegetables. Grate the carrots and one beet on the large holes of a box grater. Cut the second beet into thin matchsticks. Cube the potatoes, slice the celery, thinly slice the cabbage, and finely chop the onion.
4
When the broth has cooked for about 30 minutes, add the potatoes, celery, cabbage, and bay leaf. Continue simmering until the potatoes are tender.
5
While the potatoes cook, heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-low heat. Add the onion, carrots, and garlic and sauté until fragrant and beginning to soften.
6
Add the beets and tomato paste to the skillet. Cook for about 8 minutes, stirring frequently, until the vegetables soften and the tomato paste deepens in color.
7
Reduce the soup to medium-low heat. Stir in the cooked beet mixture and lemon juice. Simmer gently for 5 minutes.
8
Season with black pepper and the remaining salt as needed. Stir in the parsley or dill, remove the bay leaf, and serve hot with sour cream and bread.

💡 Cook's Tips

  • Choose flavorful beef: Beef soup bone, chuck roast, stew meat, or top sirloin all work well.
  • Skim the broth early: Removing the foam is optional for flavor but produces a clearer finished soup.
  • Vary the beet texture: Grating one beet and cutting the other into matchsticks gives the soup both color and texture.
  • Cook the tomato paste well: Letting it darken with the beets builds a deeper, less raw tomato flavor.
  • Cabbage options: Green, purple, or Napa cabbage can be used; Napa cooks especially quickly.
  • Storage: Refrigerate for up to 5 days or freeze for up to 3 months. Reheat gently to preserve the bright beet color.
