🥒 Spicy Cucumbers with Mint, Scallions and Crushed Peanuts

A refreshing and tangy Asian-inspired salad with a kick of spice and crunch. Original Recipe

English Español
← Back to Recipes
Prep Time 15 minutes
Cook Time 0 minutes
Total Time 15 minutes
Servings 4

Ingredients

  • 3 pounds cucumbers (about 6-8 medium), cut into spears or chunks
  • 3 tablespoons rice vinegar
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon sesame oil
  • 1 tablespoon honey or sugar
  • 2-3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1-2 teaspoons chili flakes (or to taste)
  • 1/4 cup roasted peanuts, coarsely crushed
  • 1/3 cup fresh mint leaves, torn
  • 4 scallions, chopped (white and light green parts)
  • Juice of 1/2 lime (about 1 tablespoon)
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

1
Prepare the cucumbers by cutting them into spears or 2-inch chunks. If using large cucumbers, scoop out some of the seeds to reduce excess moisture.
2
In a small bowl, whisk together the rice vinegar, soy sauce, sesame oil, honey, minced garlic, and chili flakes. Taste and adjust the seasoning.
3
Place the cucumber pieces in a large bowl. Pour the dressing over the cucumbers and toss to coat evenly.
4
Let the cucumbers sit for 5-10 minutes to allow the flavors to meld and the cucumbers to release some liquid.
5
Just before serving, add the lime juice, crushed peanuts, torn mint leaves, and chopped scallions. Toss gently to combine.
6
Taste and adjust seasoning with salt, pepper, or additional chili flakes as desired. Serve immediately or chilled.

💡 Chef's Tips

  • Cucumber Selection: Use firm, fresh cucumbers. English cucumbers have fewer seeds and are ideal, but regular ones work too.
  • Reducing Moisture: Lightly salt the cucumber pieces and let them sit for 10 minutes, then pat dry before dressing to prevent a watery salad.
  • Spice Level: Adjust chili flakes to your preference. Start with 1 teaspoon and add more for extra heat.
  • Make Ahead: Prepare the dressing up to 4 hours ahead. Add the fresh herbs and peanuts just before serving.
  • Peanut Alternatives: Use crushed cashews, almonds, or sesame seeds for a different flavor profile.
  • Herb Substitutions: Cilantro, Thai basil, or regular basil work well instead of mint.
  • Storage: Best served fresh, but can be stored covered in the refrigerator for up to 2 days.
