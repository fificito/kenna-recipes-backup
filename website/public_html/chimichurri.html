🌿 Chimichurri

A bold parsley, oregano, garlic and árbol chile sauce that becomes more flavorful as it rests. Source: Milagrosa on Facebook · User-provided recipe document.

Ingredients

  • 2 bunches fresh parsley, very finely chopped by hand
  • ¼ cup dried oregano
  • 1 tablespoon ground black pepper
  • ½ tablespoon salt
  • 3 garlic cloves, very finely chopped
  • 5–6 dried árbol chiles, chopped into small pieces
  • Warm water, as needed for hydrating
  • ⅓ cup wine vinegar
  • ⅔ cup sunflower oil

Instructions

1
Place the oregano and chopped dried chiles in a deep glass dish.
2
Add enough warm water to hydrate them. Stir well and let them absorb the water until softened.
3
Add the salt, black pepper and finely chopped garlic. Continue stirring.
4
Add all the parsley and mix thoroughly.
5
Pour in the wine vinegar, followed by the sunflower oil, and stir until evenly combined.
6
Transfer to a clean glass jar, cover and refrigerate. The flavor will deepen as it rests.

💡 Tips

  • Chop the parsley by hand: The source specifically says not to use a blender for the parsley.
  • Chile safety: If using a blender to break up the dried chiles, cover it very securely to keep chile dust out of the air.
  • Keep the ratio: Use twice as much oil as vinegar.
  • Storage: Keep refrigerated in a clean glass jar. Use clean utensils when serving.
