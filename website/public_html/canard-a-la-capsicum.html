🦆 Canard à la Capsicum

Roast duck baked over orange slices and basted with a caramelized orange gastrique. User-provided French recipe document.

Prep TimeNot specified
Cook Time3–4 hours
Total Time3–4 hours plus prep
Servings6

Ingredients

  • 1 whole duck
  • 5 oranges
  • 200 g granulated sugar
  • 3 tablespoons wine vinegar
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste

Instructions

1
Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
2
Make a caramel gastrique with the sugar and wine vinegar, heating until the mixture caramelizes.
3
Juice 2 oranges. Carefully add the orange juice to the caramelized mixture while stirring.
4
Slice the remaining 3 oranges and line the bottom of an ovenproof dish with the slices. Set the duck on top.
5
Pour the orange gastrique over the duck, then season lightly with salt and pepper.
6
Roast for 3–4 hours, basting the duck regularly with the pan juices.
7
The duck should develop a dark red-brown color by the end of cooking. Watch the orange slices so they do not caramelize excessively.

💡 Cook's Notes

  • Original title retained: The supplied document is titled “Canard à la Capsicum,” although its ingredients and method are orange-based.
  • Add juice carefully: Hot caramel may splatter when liquid is added; pour slowly and stir with care.
  • Baste regularly: Frequent basting builds the dark, glossy finish described in the source.
  • Check doneness: Cooking time varies with duck size; verify the thickest parts are fully cooked before serving.
