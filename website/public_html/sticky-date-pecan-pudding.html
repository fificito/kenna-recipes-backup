🍮 Sticky Date and Pecan Pudding

A warm self-saucing date cake with roasted pecans, aromatic spice and bubbling toffee sauce, balanced by tangy date-molasses sour cream. Original recipe by Yotam Ottolenghi for NYT Cooking

Prep Time20 minutes
Cook Time40 minutes
Total Time1 hour
Servings8

Ingredients

  • 8 ounces pitted dates, finely chopped, about 1 packed cup
  • 2¾ cups boiling water, divided
  • ¾ cup (170 g) unsalted butter, cut into ½-inch cubes and softened, divided
  • 1¼ packed cups (305 g) dark brown sugar, divided
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1¾ cups (235 g) all-purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 2 teaspoons pumpkin spice mix
  • 1½ teaspoons flaky sea salt
  • ¾ cup roughly chopped pecans
  • 4 tablespoons date molasses, divided
  • 1 cup sour cream

Instructions

1
Combine the chopped dates with ¾ cup boiling water in a heatproof bowl. Let stand for 10 minutes, then mash the dates and their soaking liquid with a fork into a rough paste.
2
Using an electric mixer on medium speed, beat ½ cup butter with ½ cup brown sugar until pale and fluffy, about 5 minutes, scraping the bowl halfway through.
3
Beat in the eggs, then fold in the date paste. Add the flour, baking powder, pumpkin spice and flaky salt; fold just until evenly combined.
4
Heat the oven to 350°F. Spread the batter evenly in a high-sided 9-by-13-inch baking dish.
5
Scatter the pecans over the batter. Drizzle with 3 tablespoons date molasses, then distribute the remaining ¾ cup brown sugar and ¼ cup butter over the top.
6
Carefully pour the remaining 2 cups boiling water over the assembled pudding. Bake for 35–40 minutes, until the center is set and caramel bubbles around the sides.
7
Remove from the oven and let the pudding rest for 10 minutes.
8
Lightly swirl the remaining tablespoon of date molasses through the sour cream. Serve the warm pudding with the flavored sour cream alongside.

💡 Cook's Tips

  • Keep the soaking water: Mash the softened dates together with their liquid; do not drain them.
  • Use a high-sided dish: The cake rises while the water, butter and sugar form a bubbling sauce underneath.
  • Make ahead: Assemble through the pecan, molasses, sugar and butter topping, then refrigerate for up to 8 hours. Add the boiling water immediately before baking.
  • Do not overmix: Fold the dry ingredients only until combined to keep the cake light.
  • Rest before serving: Ten minutes allows the bubbling sauce to settle while the pudding remains warm.
  • Serve fresh: The pudding is at its best soon after baking, with the tangy sour cream balancing its sweetness.
