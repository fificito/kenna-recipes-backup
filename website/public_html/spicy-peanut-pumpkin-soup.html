🥣 Spicy Peanut and Pumpkin Soup

A warm, silky pumpkin soup inspired by West African flavors, with peanut butter, coconut milk, ginger, and fragrant chile. Original recipe by Yewande Komolafe for NYT Cooking

Prep TimeNot specified
Cook TimeNot specified
Total Time35 minutes
Servings4

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 medium onion, peeled and diced, about 1½ cups
  • 4 garlic cloves, smashed
  • 1-inch piece fresh ginger, peeled and chopped
  • ½ habanero or bird's eye chile
  • 1 (14-ounce) can pumpkin purée
  • 3 cups water, chicken stock, or vegetable stock
  • 1 (13-ounce) can coconut milk
  • 1 tablespoon agave or honey, optional
  • ¼ cup unsweetened natural peanut butter
  • Salt, to taste
  • 2 tablespoons sliced fresh chives, optional
  • ¼ cup crème fraîche or plain yogurt, optional
  • Olive oil for drizzling, optional vegan garnish
  • Warm baguette or sourdough bread, for serving

Instructions

1
Heat the olive oil in a large stockpot over medium heat. Add the onion, garlic, and ginger. Cook, stirring frequently, until softened and beginning to brown around the edges, about 4 minutes.
2
Stir in the chile and pumpkin purée, then whisk in the water or stock. Bring to a boil, reduce the heat, and simmer gently for 20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until slightly reduced and thickened.
3
For a milder soup, remove the chile after simmering. Leave it in for more heat.
4
Add the coconut milk, agave or honey if using, and peanut butter. Purée until smooth with an immersion blender, or carefully work in batches in a countertop blender.
5
Season with salt and keep warm over low heat. Do not let the soup return to a simmer or boil, which could cause the peanut butter oils to separate and affect the smooth texture.
6
Divide among bowls. Garnish with chives and crème fraîche or yogurt, or use a drizzle of olive oil for a vegan finish. Serve with warm crusty bread for dipping.

💡 Cook's Tips

  • Adjust the chile: Habanero gives the soup fragrance as well as heat. Remove it before blending for a gentler result.
  • Keep it vegan: Use vegetable stock, agave, and an olive-oil garnish instead of honey or dairy.
  • Protect the texture: Once the peanut butter is added, warm the soup gently without boiling.
  • Round out the flavor: The optional agave or honey softens the chile and balances the savory peanut flavor.
  • Blend safely: If using a countertop blender, process the hot soup in small batches and vent the lid.
  • Source timing: The original recipe specifies a 35-minute total but does not list separate prep and cook times.
