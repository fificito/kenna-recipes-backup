🍹 Mezcalita

A smoky, tart and floral mezcal cocktail with lemon, agave and hibiscus concentrate, finished with sal de gusano and rosemary. Original recipe by Héctor Palacios for Sunset Magazine

Prep TimeNot specified

Total TimeNot specified
Servings1 cocktail

Ingredients

  • 1 ounce lemon juice
  • 1 ounce agave syrup
  • 1½ ounces hibiscus (jamaica) concentrate
  • 1½ ounces mezcal
  • Sal de gusano, for the rim
  • 1 lemon slice, for garnish
  • 1 sprig rosemary, for garnish
  • Ice cubes

Instructions

1
Coat the rim of a rocks glass with sal de gusano and set the glass aside.
2
Add the lemon juice, agave syrup, hibiscus concentrate and mezcal to a cocktail shaker filled with ice.
3
Shake vigorously until frost forms on the outside of the shaker.
4
Strain into the prepared rocks glass. Garnish with the lemon slice and rosemary sprig.
5
For a smoky presentation, carefully singe the rosemary with a match or kitchen torch, if desired, then serve immediately.

💡 Bartender's Tips

  • Chill thoroughly: Shake hard enough for the metal shaker to become frosty.
  • Balance the sweetness: Stir the agave syrup before measuring and use fresh lemon juice for brightness.
  • Prepare the rim first: Rim the empty glass before mixing so the drink is served immediately after shaking.
  • Handle flame carefully: If smoking the rosemary, keep the glass away from other flammable items and extinguish the sprig before drinking.
  • Sal de gusano: Apply a light, even rim so its savory chile flavor complements rather than overwhelms the cocktail.
