🥜 Three-Ingredient Peanut Butter Cookies

Tender, deeply peanutty, flourless cookies made with only peanut butter, sugar, and an egg. Original recipe by Ali Slagle for NYT Cooking

English Español
← Back to Recipes
Prep Time5 minutes
Cook Time20 minutes
Total Time25 minutes
Yield10 cookies

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (270 g) smooth peanut butter, preferably conventional rather than natural
  • ½ cup (99 g) granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg

Instructions

1
Heat the oven to 350°F. Line a sheet pan with parchment paper.
2
In a medium bowl, stir together the peanut butter, sugar, and egg until combined. Stir vigorously for 20 seconds, until the dough thickens and becomes smooth.
3
Using a 2-tablespoon cookie or ice-cream scoop, portion the dough into about 10 cookies, approximately 40 grams each. Arrange them on the prepared sheet pan.
4
For firmer, less fudgy cookies, gently press a crosshatch pattern into the top of each portion with a fork.
5
Bake for 13–16 minutes, until puffed and slightly darker around the edges. The centers may still look underdone.
6
Let the cookies cool completely on the sheet pan, where they will firm up as they cool.

💡 Cook's Tips

  • Peanut butter choice: Conventional smooth peanut butter gives the most predictable texture, though natural peanut butter can also work.
  • Do not overbake: The cookies may look slightly underdone when they are ready; they continue setting on the hot pan.
  • Choose the texture: Leave the scoops rounded for softer, fudgier centers, or flatten them with a fork for firmer cookies.
  • Optional vanilla: Add ½ teaspoon vanilla extract for extra aroma.
  • Optional finish: Sprinkle the tops lightly with flaky sea salt before baking.
  • Flourless recipe: Confirm that every packaged ingredient meets your dietary requirements if preparing these for someone avoiding gluten.
