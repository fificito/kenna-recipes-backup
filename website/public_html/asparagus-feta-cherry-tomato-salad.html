🥗 Asparagus, Feta and Cherry Tomato Salad

A fresh single-serving salad with crisp-tender asparagus, juicy cherry tomatoes, feta, walnuts and a simple balsamic-mustard dressing. The preparation is a best-effort reconstruction because the original photo did not include its instructions. Recipe photo supplied by Philippe Maurer

Ingredients

  • 225 g green asparagus
  • 75 g cherry tomatoes
  • ½ garlic clove
  • 1 tablespoon balsamic vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon olive, sunflower or coconut oil
  • ½ teaspoon mustard
  • 10 g reduced-fat feta or vegan feta
  • 10 g walnuts
  • Salt, to taste

Instructions

1
Trim away the woody ends of the asparagus and cut the tender spears into bite-size pieces.
2
For crisp-tender asparagus, place the pieces in boiling salted water for 2–3 minutes. Drain immediately and cool under cold running water, then pat dry. If the asparagus is very young and tender, it may instead be sliced thinly and used raw.
3
Halve the cherry tomatoes, finely mince the garlic, crumble the feta and roughly chop the walnuts.
4
In a small bowl, whisk together the balsamic vinegar, oil, mustard, garlic and a small pinch of salt until combined.
5
Place the asparagus and tomatoes in a serving bowl. Add the dressing and toss gently until evenly coated.
6
Scatter the feta and walnuts over the salad. Taste, add more salt only if needed, and serve immediately.

💡 Cook's Tips

  • Do not overcook the asparagus: It should remain bright green and slightly crisp.
  • Dry it well: Excess water can dilute the balsamic dressing.
  • Toast the walnuts: A brief toast in a dry skillet adds flavor and crunch; let them cool before adding.
  • Salt carefully: Feta is naturally salty, so taste the finished salad before adding more.
  • Vegan option: Use the vegan feta alternative shown in the source ingredients.
