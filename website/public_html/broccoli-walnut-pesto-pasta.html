🥦 Broccoli Walnut Pesto Pasta

A delicious and healthy pasta dish with fresh broccoli and homemade walnut pesto. Original Recipe

English Español
← Back to Recipes
Prep Time 10 minutes
Cook Time 20 minutes
Total Time 30 minutes
Servings 4

For the Broccoli Walnut Pesto

  • 1 cup fresh basil leaves
  • 1/2 cup walnuts
  • 2 cups fresh broccoli florets
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1/2 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/3 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
  • Salt and pepper to taste

For the Pasta

  • 1 pound pasta (penne, fusilli, or your favorite shape)
  • 2 cups fresh broccoli florets
  • Salt for pasta water
  • Reserved pasta water (about 1/2 cup)
  • Extra Parmesan cheese for serving
  • Red pepper flakes (optional)

Instructions

1
Make the pesto: Add the basil, walnuts, broccoli florets, and garlic to a food processor. Pulse until coarsely chopped.
2
While pulsing, slowly drizzle in the olive oil until you reach your desired consistency. Transfer to a bowl and stir in the Parmesan cheese and lemon juice. Season with salt and pepper. Set aside.
3
Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the pasta and cook according to package directions, about 2 minutes before it's completely done. Add the broccoli florets to the pot and cook together for the remaining 2 minutes.
4
Reserve about 1/2 cup of pasta water, then drain the pasta and broccoli.
5
Return the drained pasta and broccoli to the pot. Add the pesto and toss to coat, adding pasta water a little at a time to achieve your desired consistency.
6
Taste and adjust seasoning. Serve immediately with extra Parmesan cheese and a sprinkle of red pepper flakes if desired.

💡 Chef's Tips

  • Broccoli in Pesto: Using broccoli in the pesto itself adds fresh flavor and reduces the amount of basil and nuts needed.
  • Walnut vs. Pine Nuts: Walnuts are more budget-friendly than pine nuts and add a slightly earthier flavor to the pesto.
  • Pasta Water is Magic: The starchy pasta water helps create a silky sauce. Add it gradually to get the right consistency.
  • Fresh Ingredients: Use fresh basil for the best flavor. If not available, you can substitute 2 tablespoons of store-bought pesto.
  • Cook Pasta Al Dente: Cook the pasta slightly less than directed to avoid mushiness when combined with the pesto.
  • Make Ahead: The pesto can be made up to 2 days in advance and stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator.
  • Protein Addition: Add grilled chicken, toasted pine nuts, or crispy chickpeas for extra protein.
