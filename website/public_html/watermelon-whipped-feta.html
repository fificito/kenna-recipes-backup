🍉 Watermelon With Whipped Feta

Juicy watermelon dressed with lemon and salt, served over silky whipped feta and yogurt with fresh herbs and olive oil. Original recipe by Ali Slagle for NYT Cooking

← Back to Recipes
Prep Time5 minutes
Assembly5 minutes
Total Time10 minutes
Servings4

Ingredients

  • 8 ounces feta, drained
  • ¾ cup full-fat Greek yogurt (one 5-ounce container)
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 2 pounds seedless watermelon, cut into 1- to 2-inch pieces
  • Salt, preferably flaky
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • Leaves from 2 mint or basil sprigs
  • Crushed red pepper, for serving, optional

Instructions

1
In a food processor or blender, combine the feta, Greek yogurt, and lemon zest. Blend until very smooth and fluffy.
2
If the feta mixture is too thick to blend smoothly, add water or more yogurt 1 tablespoon at a time until it resembles thick whipped cream. Spread it over a large platter.
3
In a large bowl, combine the watermelon, lemon juice, and a pinch of salt. Stir gently to coat.
4
Arrange the watermelon over the whipped feta, leaving the accumulated watermelon juices in the bowl.
5
Drizzle with olive oil and finish with mint or basil leaves and crushed red pepper, if using. Serve immediately.

💡 Cook's Tips

  • Drain the feta: Removing excess brine keeps the whipped base rich and creamy instead of watery.
  • Adjust the texture: Add water or yogurt gradually so the whipped feta stays thick enough to support the fruit.
  • Leave the juices behind: Use a slotted spoon or drain the watermelon briefly to prevent the platter from becoming soupy.
  • Serve right away: Salt draws moisture from watermelon, so assemble the dish immediately before eating.
  • Optional additions: Arugula, sliced cucumber, or tomatoes can be tossed with the watermelon before plating.
