🍎 Classic Waldorf Salad

A crisp, creamy salad with tart apples, celery, grapes, and toasted nuts. Original Recipe

English Español
← Back to Recipes
Prep Time10 minutes
Chill Time1 hour
Total Time1 hour 10 minutes
Servings6

Ingredients

  • 2 cups diced tart apples (about 2 apples)
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 2 teaspoons lemon juice
  • 1 cup diced celery
  • 1 cup grapes, halved
  • ½ cup toasted walnuts or pecans, roughly chopped
  • ½ cup whipping cream, whipped
  • ¼ cup mayonnaise
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt
  • Lettuce leaves, for serving (optional)

Instructions

1
Place the diced apples in a large mixing bowl. Sprinkle with the sugar and lemon juice, then toss well to coat. Add the celery, halved grapes, and toasted nuts.
2
In a separate bowl, whip the cream until peaks form. Gently fold in the mayonnaise, taking care not to deflate the cream.
3
Fold the creamy dressing into the apple mixture and season with kosher salt. Taste and adjust the seasoning. Cover and chill for 1 hour or overnight, then serve on its own or over lettuce leaves.

💡 Chef's Tips

  • Choose tart, crisp apples: Granny Smith apples hold their shape and balance the sweet grapes and creamy dressing.
  • Prevent browning: Toss the apples with lemon juice immediately after cutting.
  • Toast the nuts: Toasting walnuts or pecans deepens their flavor and keeps the salad crunchy.
  • Fold gently: The whipped cream makes the dressing light. Fold rather than stir vigorously.
  • Make ahead: Chilling allows the flavors to blend. The salad can be prepared the night before serving.
  • Nutrition: Approximately 245 calories per serving.
