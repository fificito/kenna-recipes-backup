🍄 Mushroom Risotto

A creamy porcini and chestnut mushroom risotto with white wine, Parmesan and parsley. The supplied ingredient list was completed with the method and finishing ingredients from the original Good Food recipe · User-provided partial document.

Prep Time5 minutes
Cook Time25 minutes, plus soaking
Total TimeAbout 50 minutes
Servings4

Ingredients

  • 50 g dried porcini mushrooms
  • 1 litre boiling water
  • 1 vegetable stock cube
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • 250 g chestnut mushrooms, chopped
  • 300 g risotto rice, such as Arborio
  • 175 ml white wine
  • 25 g butter
  • Handful parsley leaves, chopped
  • 50 g Parmesan or Grana Padano, freshly grated
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Instructions

1
Put the dried porcini in a large bowl and pour over 1 litre boiling water. Soak for 20 minutes, then drain into another bowl, leaving the last few tablespoons of gritty liquid behind.
2
Crumble the stock cube into the reserved mushroom liquid. Gently squeeze excess liquid from the porcini, then chop them.
3
Heat the olive oil in a shallow saucepan or deep frying pan over medium heat. Add the onion and garlic and cook for about 5 minutes, until soft.
4
Stir in the chestnut mushrooms and chopped porcini. Season with salt and pepper and cook for 8 minutes, until the fresh mushrooms soften.
5
Add the rice and cook for 1 minute. Pour in the wine and let it bubble until it has evaporated.
6
Add one-quarter of the mushroom stock. Simmer, stirring often, until absorbed.
7
Continue adding the stock a quarter at a time, stirring and allowing each addition to absorb. The rice should become creamy and tender.
8
When the rice is cooked, remove from the heat. Stir in the butter, half the Parmesan and half the parsley. Add a splash of water if the rice is still too firm.
9
Cover and rest for a few minutes. Stir again, spoon into bowls and finish with the remaining Parmesan and parsley.

💡 Cook's Tips

  • Avoid grit: Leave the last few tablespoons of porcini soaking liquid behind.
  • Add stock gradually: Frequent stirring helps release the rice starch and create a creamy texture.
  • Serve promptly: Risotto thickens as it sits; loosen with a splash of hot water if needed.
  • Vegetarian option: Use a vegetarian Parmesan-style cheese if required.
