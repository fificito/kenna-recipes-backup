🥗 Chickpea and Beet Salad with Feta

A colorful Mediterranean-style salad with protein-rich chickpeas, earthy beets, creamy feta, and a bright lemon-garlic vinaigrette. Original recipe from Easy Cook Plates · Shared Pinterest pin

Prep Time10 minutes
Cook TimeNo cooking
Total Time10 minutes, plus optional chilling
Servings4

For the Salad

  • 1 can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 2 cups cooked beets, chopped
  • 1 cup feta cheese, crumbled
  • ½ cup red onion, thinly sliced
  • ¼ cup fresh parsley, chopped

For the Lemon-Garlic Vinaigrette

  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

Instructions

1
Combine the chickpeas, chopped beets, feta, red onion, and parsley in a large bowl.
2
In a small bowl, whisk together the olive oil, lemon juice, minced garlic, salt, and pepper to make the vinaigrette.
3
Pour the vinaigrette over the salad and toss gently until evenly combined.
4
Serve immediately, or refrigerate for about 30 minutes before serving for a chilled salad.

💡 Cook's Tips

  • Dry the ingredients: Drain the chickpeas and beets well so the vinaigrette stays bright instead of watery.
  • Toss gently: Beets will tint the feta pink; fold carefully if you prefer more distinct colors.
  • Balance the dressing: Taste after adding the feta, which contributes salt, before adding more seasoning.
  • Meal prep: The salad can be chilled before serving, but add some feta at the end for a fresher presentation.
  • Use cooked beets: Roasted, steamed, or vacuum-packed cooked beets all work well.
