🥬 Provençal Greens Soup

A simple French soup of tender mixed greens, leeks, garlic, and egg, served over crisp garlic-rubbed country bread. Original recipe by Martha Rose Shulman for NYT Cooking

Prep TimeNot specified
Cook TimeNot specified
Total Time45 minutes
Servings4

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil, divided
  • 2 leeks, halved lengthwise, sliced, thoroughly rinsed, and drained
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  • Kosher salt, to taste
  • 6 cups chopped leafy greens, leaves only, such as Swiss chard, dandelion greens, watercress, and beet greens
  • 6 cups water
  • Freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • 2 large eggs
  • 4 thick slices country bread, toasted
  • 1 garlic clove, halved, for rubbing the toast
  • Grated Parmesan, for serving, optional

Instructions

1
Heat 1 tablespoon olive oil in a large, heavy soup pot over medium heat. Add the leeks and cook, stirring, until tender, 3–5 minutes.
2
Add the sliced garlic and ½ teaspoon salt. Cook, stirring, until fragrant, about 1 minute.
3
Add the greens and stir until they begin to wilt. Pour in 6 cups water, season with salt, and bring to a simmer.
4
Reduce the heat and simmer, partially covered, for 15–20 minutes, until the greens are very tender and the broth tastes sweet. Add black pepper, taste, and adjust the seasoning.
5
Beat the eggs in a bowl. Make sure the soup is no longer boiling, then gradually whisk one ladleful of hot soup into the eggs to temper them.
6
Remove the soup from the heat and slowly stir in the tempered egg mixture.
7
Rub the toasted country bread with the cut garlic clove and brush with the remaining olive oil. Place one or two pieces in each bowl.
8
Ladle the soup over the toast. Sprinkle with Parmesan, if desired, and serve immediately.

💡 Cook's Tips

  • Choose your greens: A mix gives the most interesting flavor, but Swiss chard works especially well on its own.
  • Wash leeks carefully: Dirt often hides between their layers, so rinse the slices thoroughly.
  • Temper the eggs: Whisk hot broth into the eggs gradually and keep the soup below a boil to prevent scrambling.
  • Make ahead: The soup can be prepared through the greens-and-broth stage several hours before serving. Reheat gently before adding the eggs.
  • Use sturdy bread: Thick country bread holds its shape while soaking up the flavorful broth.
  • Source timing: The original recipe specifies a 45-minute total but does not list separate prep and cook times.
