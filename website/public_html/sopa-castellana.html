🧄 Sopa Castellana (Spanish Garlic Soup)

Felipe Maurer's light garlic-soup method, with fried stale bread and an egg cooked in the piping-hot broth. Read the original supplied method. Traditional context checked against the official Castilla-La Mancha recipe.

ServingsFlexible; 1 egg per serving

Ingredients

  • 6–8 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • Olive oil, as needed for gently frying
  • Chicken broth, or water with chicken bouillon, as needed
  • Stale bread, cut into slices
  • 1 egg per serving
  • Salt, if needed
  • Spanish paprika, optional for the traditional thickened variation

Instructions

1
Heat a generous film of olive oil in a saucepan over very low heat. Add the thinly sliced garlic.
2
Cook gently until the garlic just begins to turn golden. Do not let it burn.
3
Add chicken broth, or water seasoned with chicken bouillon. Bring to a gentle boil and cook for about 15 minutes.
4
Meanwhile, fry the stale bread slices separately in a little olive oil until golden.
5
Put fried bread in each serving bowl. Ladle the boiling garlic broth and sliced garlic over the bread.
6
Immediately crack one egg into each bowl. Let it cook in the very hot broth until the white is set to your liking, then serve.

💡 Traditional Variation & Tips

  • Castilian variation: Fry the garlic gently, remove the pot briefly from the heat, stir in paprika so it does not burn, then add the stale bread to the pot. Add broth and simmer until the bread partly breaks down and thickens the soup. Add the egg at the end.
  • Low heat matters: Garlic becomes bitter if it browns too far.
  • Serve immediately: The broth needs to be piping hot to cook the egg in the bowl.
  • Quantities: The supplied family method specifies the garlic and one egg per serving but leaves the broth, bread and oil flexible.
